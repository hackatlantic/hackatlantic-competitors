# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

HackAtlantic is a student community. We expect contributors, reviewers, event staff, and maintainers to be respectful, constructive, and inclusive. Assume good intent, discuss ideas rather than people, and make room for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, specific, and professional in issues, pull requests, chats, and event operations.
- Welcome questions and explain decisions without belittling someone.
- Respect privacy. Applicant records, credentials, and internal operational details are not conversation material.
- Give feedback on the work, not a person’s identity, ability, or background.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, sexualised content in community spaces, doxxing, or sharing private applicant/staff data without authorisation are unacceptable. So is knowingly attempting to bypass access controls or exposing credentials.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report an incident privately to a HackAtlantic organiser or repository maintainer. Include only the facts needed to investigate; do not circulate sensitive details publicly. Maintainers may ask for clarification, remove content, limit participation, or take other proportionate action to keep the community safe. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern is not acceptable.

This policy applies to this repository, HackAtlantic-operated online spaces, and related events.
